Features

The best deals, without the scrolling.

A full deal workspace that opens on top of jiomart.com, in one click.

Biggest discounts first

Shows the highest discounts across everything you scanned, then the best of each category. No searching by hand.

Price-drop tracking

Every scan is remembered. New products, cheaper prices and lowest-ever prices get a label.

You choose what to scan

Each scan asks which categories to check, so you only scan what you care about.

Telegram alerts

New deals, price drops and cheaper saved items, sent straight to your phone.

Smart shopping list

Quantities, total and total savings. Copy it or open every item at once.

Private by design

Runs in your own session. No servers, no tracking, no account.

Self-test & diagnostics

A Self-test button runs the real parser on built-in samples with no network, and a scan that finds nothing names the cause — no pincode, signed out, rate-limited, or everything filtered out.

Deep scan

One tap scans all 20 categories, 5 pages each, in parallel, with a live per-page progress readout — and results are de-duplicated across pages and categories.

Best value per kg / litre

Beside the biggest-%-off grid, a best-value row ranks the cheapest unit price across every category scanned, so a cheap staple is not buried by a high-%-off snack.

In stock only, by default

Out-of-stock items are hidden unless you switch the filter off, so the grid only shows deals you can actually buy.

FAQ

Good questions.

Is it safe? Does it see my data?

It runs inside your browser tab using your own JioMart session. There is no server and no analytics. Saved items and price history stay in your browser. The only exception is Telegram alerts: if you switch them on, the deals you choose are sent to your own bot — nothing else leaves the browser.

Why do I get no results at first?

JioMart needs a delivery pincode. Open jiomart.com, set your pincode, then scan again.

Is it official?

No. It is an independent tool, not affiliated with JioMart. It only reads what your account can already see.

Will it keep working?

JioMart can change its internal APIs at any time. The parser reads responses loosely to survive small changes, and the tool tells you when a scan returns nothing.

What is new in v2 (8 November 2026)?

The fetch layer now uses JioMart's current Vertex product endpoint with store routing from your pincode, falling back to the older catalogue endpoint, and prices are read max-first — fixing scans that fetched products but showed a blank grid. There is also a standalone Python Telegram bot in bot-v2/ that alerts around the clock. v2.1 added empty-state diagnostics that name the cause of 0 results, a built-in Self-test, retry with backoff, deeper scans, a best-value-per-kg row, and the highest-discount fix — genuine 96–99% off items now render instead of being dropped as inflated prices. See the README and CHANGELOG in the repo.

How does price-drop tracking work?

After each scan the tool remembers every price in your browser. Next time, new products are labelled New and cheaper ones Price drop.

Where does the data come from?

Live from JioMart, inside your own logged-in tab and for your delivery address. Nothing is stored on any server; saved items and price history stay in your browser.
